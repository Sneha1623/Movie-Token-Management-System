About the Movie

Ikkis is an upcoming Indian war biographical drama directed by acclaimed filmmaker Sriram Raghavan. Set against the backdrop of the **Indo-Pakistani War of 1971**, the film is inspired by the real-life story of one of India’s youngest and bravest war heroes, Second Lieutenant Arun Khetarpal. The story follows the remarkable journey of a 21-year-old officer whose courage, determination, and unwavering sense of duty lead him into one of the most intense battles of the war—the Battle of Basantar. Coming from a disciplined military background, he embodies the spirit of fearless leadership as he faces overwhelming enemy forces with unshakable resolve. As the narrative unfolds, Ikkis captures not only the adrenaline-filled moments of battlefield combat but also the emotional depth of a young soldier’s life, his values, and the sacrifices that define true heroism. His journey transforms him into a timeless symbol of bravery and patriotism. Featuring a powerful performance by Varun Dhawan, the film combines gripping storytelling, realistic war sequences, and heartfelt emotion. Ikkis stands as a tribute to courage, sacrifice, and the enduring spirit of those who lay down their lives for the nation, aiming to leave a lasting impact on audiences while honoring a real-life legend.
