About the Movie

RRR (Rise Roar Revolt) is an epic Indian action drama directed by visionary filmmaker S. S. Rajamouli. Set in the 1920s during British colonial rule, the film is a fictional tale inspired by the lives of two legendary revolutionaries—Alluri Sitarama Raju and Komaram Bheem. The story follows the powerful journey of two men from completely different backgrounds who cross paths and form an extraordinary bond of friendship. As their destinies unfold, they rise against oppression, transforming into symbols of resistance and courage. Packed with breathtaking action sequences, emotionally charged storytelling, and grand visuals, RRR celebrates unity, sacrifice, and the indomitable spirit of freedom. With standout performances by Ram Charan and N. T. Rama Rao Jr., and a globally acclaimed soundtrack featuring the Oscar-winning song Naatu Naatu, RRR has become a worldwide cinematic phenomenon, redefining the scale and reach of Indian cinema.
